Benefit Availment Process

Members are encouraged to verify dentist availability, confirm their benefits, and prepare the required identification before their visit.

1
support_agent

Check Your Dental Benefits and Find an Accredited Dentist

Contact the DNC Hotline or use the Search Dentist/Clinic feature to verify your dental benefits and find an accredited dentist near you, including clinic location and schedule.

2
event_available

Schedule an Appointment

Contact your preferred accredited dentist directly to schedule an appointment before visiting the clinic.

3
verified_user

Appointment Verification

The dentist will contact the DNC Hotline to verify your eligibility and benefits, then confirm your appointment with you.

4
badge

Visit the Dentist

Visit the dental clinic at your scheduled appointment. Don’t forget to bring your Dental/HMO Card and a VALID ID. A valid ID is required to verify your identity and eligibility before receiving your dental benefits.
